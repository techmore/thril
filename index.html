ISO 9001:2015 — clause breakdown

Each clause with its requirements, mandatory documentation, and what auditors look for. Click to expand.

Clause 4 — Context of the organization Mandatory

What it requires: Determine external/internal issues relevant to your QMS, interested parties and their requirements, define the QMS scope, and establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the QMS including its processes.

  • 4.1 — Understand the organization and its context (SWOT/PESTLE analysis)
  • 4.2 — Understand needs and expectations of interested parties
  • 4.3 — Determine the scope of the QMS (documented)
  • 4.4 — QMS and its processes (process interaction map)
Mandatory: Scope of QMS Mandatory: Process interaction map Recommended: Context register

Auditor looks for: Evidence that you've identified relevant issues, interested parties, and that your QMS scope is accurate and justified. They'll ask for records of reviews.

Clause 5 — Leadership Mandatory

What it requires: Top management must demonstrate leadership and commitment, establish a quality policy, ensure quality objectives are set, assign QMS roles and responsibilities, and promote process approach and risk-based thinking.

  • 5.1 — Leadership and commitment
  • 5.2 — Quality policy (documented)
  • 5.3 — Organizational roles, responsibilities and authorities
Mandatory: Quality policy Mandatory: QMS role assignments

Auditor looks for: Evidence of management review meetings, signed quality policy, communication records showing leadership engagement. The quality policy must be appropriate and communicated.

Clause 6 — Planning Mandatory

What it requires: Plan actions to address risks and opportunities, establish measurable quality objectives, and plan changes to the QMS when needed.

  • 6.1 — Actions to address risks and opportunities (risk register)
  • 6.2 — Quality objectives and planning to achieve them
  • 6.3 — Planning of changes
Mandatory: Quality objectives (measured) Recommended: Risk register

Auditor looks for: Documented quality objectives with targets, evidence of risk assessment, action plans for risks/opportunities. Objectives must be monitored and communicated.

Clause 7 — Support Mandatory

What it requires: Ensure adequate resources, competent personnel, documented infrastructure and environment, measurement traceability, and documented information (document control).

  • 7.1 — Resources (people, infrastructure, environment)
  • 7.2 — Competence (training records)
  • 7.3 — Awareness
  • 7.4 — Communication
  • 7.5 — Documented information (document control + records control)
Mandatory: Document control procedure Mandatory: Records control procedure Recommended: Training matrix

Auditor looks for: Training records, competence evaluations, document approval/change history, legible records. Clause 7.5 is a frequent finding area.

Clause 8 — Operation Mandatory

What it requires: Plan, implement, and control operational processes including product/service requirements, design/development, externally provided products/services, production/service provision, and release of products/services.

  • 8.1 — Operational planning and control
  • 8.2 — Requirements for products and services (contract review)
  • 8.3 — Design and development (if applicable)
  • 8.4 — Control of externally provided processes/products/services
  • 8.5 — Production and service provision (including traceability, preservation)
  • 8.6 — Release of products and services
  • 8.7 — Control of nonconforming outputs (NCR process)
Mandatory: Nonconformity control procedure Mandatory: Records of nonconformities (NCRs) Recommended: Supplier evaluation records

Auditor looks for: Signed contracts/reviews, supplier evaluation records, traceability of product, NCR logs, corrective actions for nonconformities. This clause has the most process evidence.

Clause 9 — Performance evaluation Mandatory

What it requires: Monitor, measure, analyze, and evaluate QMS performance. Conduct internal audits at planned intervals and perform management reviews.

  • 9.1 — Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation (KPIs)
  • 9.2 — Internal audit (audit program, audit records)
  • 9.3 — Management review (review records, action items)
Mandatory: Internal audit procedure Mandatory: Internal audit records Mandatory: Management review records

Auditor looks for: Completed internal audit reports with findings, management review meeting minutes, KPI trend data. They will track closure of action items from previous reviews.

Clause 10 — Improvement Mandatory

What it requires: Determine and select opportunities for improvement, manage nonconformities and corrective actions, and continually improve the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the QMS.

  • 10.1 — Nonconformity and corrective action (root cause analysis)
  • 10.2 — Continual improvement
Mandatory: Corrective action procedure Mandatory: Corrective action records (CARs)

Auditor looks for: Root cause analysis on nonconformities, corrective actions that actually prevent recurrence, evidence of continual improvement. This is where the PDCA cycle closes.

Governance

Document control guidelines

Clause 7.5 requires documented information to be controlled. These conventions ensure audit-ready documentation.

Naming convention

[TYPE]-[dept]-[number]-[version]
e.g. SOP-OPS-001-v2, POL-QMS-001-v1

Version control

Each document has a version history table: date, version, author, approver, change description. Major revisions increment the version number.

Approval workflow

Draft → Review by QMS rep → Approval by process owner → Publish. Electronic signatures or email approval records accepted.

Review cycle

All QMS documents reviewed at least annually (or when processes change). Review date tracked in the document register.

Document register

Master list of all QMS documents: title, ID, version, effective date, owner, review date, status (draft/active/obsolete).

Obsolete management

Obsolete docs are marked "superseded" and retained for records. Current versions are clearly distinguished from historical.

Production

Assembly procedures & work instructions

Step-by-step assembly and building procedures for product lines. Each procedure references associated equipment, quality checkpoints, and required training (Clause 8.5 — production and service provision).

Micros — final assembly

Board mounting, connector seating, firmware load, and functional test for the Micros product line.

  1. Mount mainboard to chassis — 4× M3 screws @ 6 in·lb
  2. Seat ribbon cable connectors J1–J4 — verify latch click
  3. Install I/O shield plate — torque to spec on label
  4. Load firmware via JTAG — verify checksum match
  5. Run functional test script ft_micros_v2
  6. Inspect solder joints under magnification — 9× minimum
BOM-MIC-A2 WI-ELEC-001 QCP-MIC-001 Training: ASSY-100

KV9 — chassis build & wiring

Chassis assembly, harness routing, power supply installation, and continuity test for the KV9 enclosure.

  1. Install standoffs and backplane into chassis
  2. Route power harness per routing diagram KV9-WD-003
  3. Secure PSU bracket — 2× M4 screws
  4. Terminate DC lines to backplane — torque to 4 in·lb
  5. Perform continuity and isolation test — log results
  6. Install front bezel and label with serial number
BOM-KV9-B1 WI-MECH-002 QCP-KV9-001 Training: ASSY-200

General mechanical build — all product lines

Common mechanical assembly procedures applicable across Micros, KV9, and custom builds.

  1. Verify all components against BOM and revision level
  2. Inspect fasteners for correct thread pitch and length
  3. Apply thread-locker (blue Loctite 242) to all M3+ threaded joints
  4. Torque to spec — use calibrated torque driver only
  5. Verify ground bond < 0.1 Ω per QCP-GEN-002
  6. Complete build record and pass to QC inspection
WI-MECH-001 QCP-GEN-002 Training: ASSY-001

Custom build — per work order

Non-standard assemblies, prototype builds, or customer-specific configurations. Each follows a unique work order packet.

  1. Pull work order packet from QMS — verify BOM and rev
  2. Stage components on ESD-safe work surface
  3. Follow work-order-specific step sequence (appended to packet)
  4. Photograph critical assembly steps for traceability
  5. QC inspect per QCP-GEN-003 before sealing
