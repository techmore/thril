Manual assembly

Manual bottom-stock devices

These manual assembly jigs are purpose-built for bottom-stock assembly operations. Both stations use mechanical fixturing and operator-assisted processes — no CNC or digital control. Only jigs in this category are used for bottom-stock work.

Manual Jig A — bottom stock

Manual

Primary manual assembly jig for CCS bottom-stock production. Handles stock blank fixturing, inletting guide alignment, and buttpad drilling template. Operator feeds pre-cut stock blanks and completes all operations with hand tools guided by the jig.

Digital precision

Digital devices

CNC and digital measurement stations used for precision work on CCS stock components. These devices are not used for bottom-stock assembly — they handle upper-stock profiling, serialized engraving, and QC verification.

Digital Station A

Digital

CNC profiling station for upper-stock contouring and cheek-riser cutouts. 3-axis CNC with vacuum fixturing and tool-length probe. Runs from DXF toolpath files generated from CCS stock CAD models.
