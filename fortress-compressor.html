Fortress pancake compressor

Separate equipment from the T-1000 poly bagger. Follow this routine for the Fortress compressor only.

Daily routine

  1. End of day: switch the compressor off, drain the tank until water stops flowing, then close the drain valve.
  2. Pull the power: unplug the compressor after draining it.
  3. Morning check: confirm the tank is drained, the hose and fittings look secure, and the drain valve is closed.
  4. Power on: plug in the compressor and switch it on. Let it build pressure.
  5. Verify PSI: confirm the regulator/gauge is at the required operating pressure before running the equipment it supplies.
Stop and get assistance if the compressor will not build pressure, the gauge is not reading correctly, or a hose/fitting leaks.
